# Interview: Alex Marianetti, owner of Lockhunt

**When:** Cal Hacks 13.0, Sat Oct 24, 2026. **Who interviewed:** Diego. **Product shown:** the "We switched banks" scenario on the demo console, with Kai driving.
**Business:** Lockhunt, a locksmith company, two people. He gets "5 or 6 invoices every day".
**Consent:** Alex agreed to be named and to be used in demo videos. He was interviewed with the camera off for Parts 1 and 2; Part 3 was filmed on camera.
**Caveat for anything we write from this:** one informal interview, not a study. His willingness to pay is his statement, not a commitment.

Where we used it: README (problem section), `docs/devpost.md` (Inspiration), `docs/judge-qa.md` ("Who did you talk to?"), and the demo video (see "Clips" below). The repo may become public, so check with Alex before quoting more than what is already in those files. The one expletive in his answer to question 6 is replaced with [expletive].

## Part 1: Camera off, about him

**1. What's your business, and how many people work in it?**
My business is Lock Hunt Incorporated, and two people work in it.

**2. Who pays your bills, and who checks their work?**
Right now, I pay my bills, and honestly, I don't have anyone double-checking my work.

**3. Has a vendor ever changed their bank details on you? What did you do?**
Yes, and I didn't realize till later, which actually caused me some issues.

**4. Has an invoice or payment ever looked wrong or been a mistake? What happened?**
Yeah, I've had issues with recurring payments that I have to use to keep everything running.

**5. How do you handle employee receipts and reimbursements today?**
That's all done manually, in spreadsheets.

## Part 2: Camera off, he tries the product

**6. (Ran "We switched banks.") What do you notice, and what would you do if this showed up in your inbox?**
If this showed up in my inbox personally, I would have to track down everything, double-check all my spreadsheets and all that info manually, which [expletive] sucks. The fact that I have this: how does this gather information? Do I upload my company info through here? The fact that this has access to everything and that it can put everything I need together in a manner that lets me take care of issues like this so quickly is pretty sweet. Pretty sweet.

**7. (After the call finished and the invoice showed BLOCKED.) The system called the supplier's usual number, not the one on the invoice, and asked if they'd changed their bank. How do you feel about a call like that being made on your behalf?**
I think that's fair. I wouldn't see an issue with them calling it that way, especially if there's clearly enough information and it's for the benefit of my company. I think that would actually be useful.

**8. What would you need to see or change before you'd trust it with real money? Would you pay for it? If so, roughly what?**
What would I need to see here? Right now, what I'm thinking about is: how do I upload everything, all of my info, and how long would that take? I don't know if there's a way to streamline that or if you guys already have something in place for that, but that's just the main thing I think of.

Would I pay for this? Totally. I would obviously pay for this because it saves me money, and I would honestly pay a decent amount of my revenue each month, especially because in this business, it's literally 5 or 6 invoices every day. Problems with invoices really add up quickly. I would probably pay over 1,000 a month for this service.

## Part 3: Camera on (filmed)

**9. Tell us what you do and what worries you about paying bills.**
I have a locksmith company called Lockhunt. What worries me about paying bills is just the sheer amount of invoices that we get in this business specifically, and the organization of that and making sure everyone has paid.

**10. What did you think when you watched it stop the "we switched banks" invoice?**
I thought it was pretty incredible. Normally, what I would do if I was in a situation like that would be to go through the spreadsheets I have and then trace through all those individual places. Software that can streamline something like that is just incredibly useful, especially for a locksmith business.

**11. Who is this for, and would you use it? What would you want changed?**
I think this is for anybody who deals with lots of invoices and payments within their business, which is a lot of people. Something that I would like to see changed is the way files are uploaded. I think that could be streamlined a bit more, but overall, the software is very useful, and the utility is pretty obvious for any business owner.

## What we learned

- **The problem is real for him.** Nobody double-checks his payments, a vendor changed bank details on him and he found out late, and receipts live in spreadsheets.
- **The callback is acceptable.** He thought a call to the vendor on file was fair and useful.
- **His objection is getting data in.** He asked how it gathers information, how to upload "everything", and how long that takes (questions 6, 8, 11). Today Quintet takes photos and PDFs; bank and accounting-software connections are the first item under "Path to production" in the README.
- **Willingness to pay:** he said he'd "probably pay over 1,000 a month". One person's statement. Don't present it as demand validation.
